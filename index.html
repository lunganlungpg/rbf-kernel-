About National Statistics Day

National Statistics Day is celebrated on 29 June to honor Professor P. C. Mahalanobis and to promote statistical awareness in public life and national development.

Agriculture

Statistics drives crop forecasting, yield optimization, and precision-farming decisions.

Science

Research design, reproducibility, and valid inference rely on robust statistical methods.

Government Planning

Evidence-based policies use survey data and statistical indicators for national priorities.

Artificial Intelligence

Probability, estimation, and uncertainty quantification are foundational to AI systems.

Data Analytics

Analytics transforms data into insights for strategic decisions in every sector.

About P. C. Mahalanobis

Prasanta Chandra Mahalanobis

Born: 29 June 1893

Founder of the Indian Statistical Institute and pioneer of modern statistics in India.

Creator of the Mahalanobis Distance, a core concept in multivariate analysis.

Major Contributions: Sample surveys, statistical planning, national development, and statistical education.

Timeline

1893: Born in Kolkata.
1931: Founded Indian Statistical Institute (ISI).
1936: Introduced Mahalanobis Distance.
1950s: Led large-scale sample survey methodology in India.
National Planning Era: Advanced statistical planning for development.

The best way to predict the future is to design it with sound data and statistical reasoning.
